School of Management &
Entrepreneurship

Building India’s next generation of deep-tech entrepreneurs

The proposed School of Management & Entrepreneurship at IIT Kanpur is envisioned as India’s first dedicated academic institution focused on deep-tech entrepreneurship education.

Anchored at IIT Kanpur’s Noida Campus and connected to the Institute’s established SIIC–FIRST innovation ecosystem, SoME will bring together management education, technological fluency and venture creation within one integrated institution.

The School is designed to prepare founders, innovation leaders and technology managers who can convert scientific knowledge, intellectual property and emerging technologies into ventures capable of creating economic and societal value. Its academic model will combine business fundamentals with deep-tech understanding, product strategy, entrepreneurship and direct exposure to startup building.

SoME will not simply teach students how to manage existing organisations. It will help them understand how technology-led enterprises are imagined, validated, financed and scaled.

Building deep-tech entrepreneurs

India has strong technical talent and a rapidly growing startup ecosystem, but the pathway from engineering and research to venture creation remains fragmented.

Deep-tech entrepreneurship requires more than a promising idea. Founders must understand technology, markets, intellectual property, regulation, capital and long development cycles. SoME seeks to bridge this gap by creating a structured academic pathway from technology to enterprise.

About School of Management &
Entrepreneurship

The School will draw upon IIT Kanpur’s strengths in research, patents, technology development and startup incubation.

Through the SIIC–FIRST ecosystem, students will engage with founders, investors, industry mentors, Institute-generated technologies and live venture-building challenges. The proposed flagship MBA in Techno-Entrepreneurship will be complemented by executive programmes, undergraduate pathways in innovation and entrepreneurship, and doctoral research.

Located in Noida, the School will combine IIT Kanpur’s academic depth with access to Delhi NCR’s corporate, startup, investment and policy networks. Its long-term ambition is to become a leading institution for techno-management and entrepreneurship in India.

Philanthropic support can help establish:

  • Academic and residential infrastructure

  • Deep-tech and innovation laboratories

  • Faculty chairs and fellowships

  • Student scholarships

  • Startup seed grants

  • Industry immersion programmes

  • Entrepreneurship research and venture-building initiatives
